C Assumptions I made

The brief says: “Make decisions. If something's unclear, decide what it means, state your assumption, and keep going.” Here are mine.

Audience

The brief says
B2B healthcare executives and project leads responsible for patient communication and clinical operations. High intent.
It doesn't say
Who holds the budget, or whether they buy together or separately.
So I decided
Each video leads with one buyer lens, instead of assuming two different buying processes.

Channel & format

The brief says
A promotional video script, 90–150 seconds.
It doesn't say
Where the video will run, or in what format.
So I decided
LinkedIn feed, 4:5, autoplay with sound off and captions. Hooks appear as on-screen text in the first 3 seconds. The visual style follows F.Learning's published social videos.
