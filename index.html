The Second Moment

Executive lens~129s

Buyer question

Which multi-step home instructions need explanations designed for the moment of use, beyond complete written instructions?

Core concept

A complete written instruction needs an explanation designed for the second moment: when a patient must use it.

Narrative device

  1. 1Cold open: kitchen table, Day 3, 8 p.m.
  2. 2Rewind to a clear consultation
  3. 3Same instruction, rebuilt in the order it is used

Proof from the page

  • Kitchen-table insight
  • “The written version is complete”
  • Order written vs order used
  • A leaflet is enough for simple instructions

Audience

  2. It doesn't say

    Who holds the budget.

    One buyer lens per video, instead of guessing at two buying processes.

  1. The brief says

    A 90–150s promotional video.

  2. It doesn't say

    Where it runs, or in what format.
