D Why this direction

1

Narrative choice

The visual mystery begins with a complete page at the kitchen table, rewinds to a clear consultation, and returns to the same facts presented for use. The payoff is communication design, not distribution or a saved clinical outcome.

2

Landing-page evidence

Direct source fit: “The written version is complete”; the same preparation information in the order written versus the order used; explanation of what and why; a leaflet is enough for a simple one-off. “This work earns its cost when…” under multi-step, days-later, home-alone conditions.

3

Selection / exclusion

Keep only one patient journey, one editorial written-versus-used comparison, and a short buyer cost-fit insight. No QR/portal/visual-guide delivery story, fabricated clinical actions or outcome promises. Two source-equivalent layouts preserve medical facts.
